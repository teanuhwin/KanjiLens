About

漢字レンズ translates kanji from photos into hiragana and romaji — fully offline after the first load.

Capture or upload a photo, correct any OCR errors, then tap Convert. Results are saved locally on your device.

How It Works
Tesseract.js OCR — reads kanji from images
kuromoji.js Morphological analysis — kanji → kana
wanakana.js Kana conversion — hiragana → romaji
Service Worker Caches all assets for offline use
Privacy

No data ever leaves your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. History is stored in localStorage and can be cleared at any time.

Tips

• Good lighting and a flat, non-glare surface improve OCR accuracy significantly.

• The editable text field lets you fix any misread characters before converting.

• Works best on printed text (menus, signs, books). Handwriting has lower accuracy.
